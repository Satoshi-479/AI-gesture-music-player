🎵 Gesture Music Player

Show fingers to the camera to play songs

—
Upload songs and start the camera to begin
Volume: 70%

✋ How to use

  • 👆 1 finger → Song 1
  • ✌️ 2 fingers → Song 2
  • 🤟 3 fingers → Song 3
  • 🖖 4 fingers → Song 4
  • 🖐️ 5 fingers → Song 5
  • ⬆️ Hand up → Volume up
  • ⬇️ Hand down → Volume down

⚠️ Camera access is required. Hold your hand clearly in front of the camera with good lighting.

🎶 Upload Your Songs (up to 10)

Files are mapped to slots 1-10 in the order selected. No files? Demo songs will play instead.
